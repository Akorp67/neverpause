Who Made It

Made by a mother and son.

Menopause was a main topic at our kitchen table. So was losing sleep to night sweats, because she gets them herself. Not every night, but enough: waking up hot and sweaty around 2 or 3am, then lying there unable to fall back asleep.

And it wasn't just us. When we talked with our neighbors close by, so many of them were dealing with the same thing. This gown came out of their support and input. They wore the samples, told us what to change, and we changed it.
